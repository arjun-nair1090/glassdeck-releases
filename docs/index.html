Set up in three steps.

  1. Download Glassdeck and open it.It lives in the tray on Windows, the menu bar on a Mac or the app menu on Linux, and opens a page with a QR code.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone.Use the camera. The phone and the computer need to be on the same Wi-Fi; away from home, Tailscale connects them.
  3. Add it to your Home Screen.It then opens full screen and keeps the screen awake. Nothing comes from an app store: it works on iPhone, Android phones and tablets.

Questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no account, no subscription and no ads.

Do I need an app on my phone?

No. Glassdeck runs in your phone's browser. Adding it to the Home Screen makes it open full screen, like an app.

Which AI tools does it work with?

Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity and Gemini CLI. Turn on the ones you use in the panel's Settings, under AI tools. Typing replies into the terminal from the phone works with Claude Code on Windows.

Is it safe to run on my network?

The panel only answers phones that scanned your computer's QR code, which carries a private access key. Glassdeck never opens ports on your router, and there's no Glassdeck server in between: your phone talks straight to your computer. Notifications travel through Apple's or Google's push service, encrypted.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes. Install Tailscale on the computer and the phone and sign in to the same account; it's free for personal use. Glassdeck's pairing page then shows a QR code that works anywhere.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC".

Glassdeck isn't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

My Mac says Glassdeck can't be opened.

Click Done. Then open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Glassdeck. The first time you use them, also allow Accessibility (keyboard, trackpad and buttons), Screen Recording (the screen view) and Automation (music control) in the same place.

How do I set it up on Linux?

Open the AppImage once. It adds Glassdeck to your app menu and starts it when you log in. Sound needs PulseAudio or PipeWire, which almost every desktop has. On Wayland, the pairing page shows one command that lets Glassdeck use the keyboard and mouse, the way remote-desktop apps do.

How do updates work?

Glassdeck checks for a new version every few hours. When there is one, choose Update now in Settings on the phone, or in the tray or menu-bar menu on Windows and Mac. It installs the update and restarts by itself.

How do I uninstall it?

On Windows, open Settings, then Apps, and uninstall Glassdeck; it also removes what it added to your AI tools' settings. On a Mac, quit Glassdeck from the menu bar and move it to the Bin. On Linux, right-click Glassdeck in the app menu, choose Quit Glassdeck and delete the AppImage.
